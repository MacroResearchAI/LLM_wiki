# Wiki Overview

This wiki organizes macroeconomic research around traceable claims. Original material lives in `raw/`; source pages in `wiki/sources/` describe and cite it; entity, concept, and topic pages synthesize evidence; and analysis pages record filed answers to specific questions.

## Seed coverage

The initial pages capture Chair Jerome Powell's illustrative 2026-09-20 Jackson Hole remarks and their stated framing of the US policy rate relative to neutral. The seed topic records a data-dependent pause and uncertainty around neutral-rate estimates. It does not establish the European Central Bank's current framing: the wiki has no ECB source yet.

Use [the index](index.md) to navigate pages. The schema and citation rules are in `AGENTS.md`, and the working procedures are in `doc/workflows.md` at the repository root.
