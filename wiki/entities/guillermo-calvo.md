# Guillermo A. Calvo

## Overview

Guillermo A. Calvo was Senior Advisor in the Research Department of the [International Monetary Fund](international-monetary-fund.md) when this paper was written.

## Positions and statements

- 1993-03: With [Leonardo Leiderman](leonardo-leiderman.md) and [Carmen M. Reinhart](carmen-reinhart.md), argued that the 1990–91 renewal of capital inflows to Latin America was largely an external shock, and that policy could soften a reversal without reversing the real appreciation. Source: [Calvo, Leiderman, and Reinhart, capital inflows (1993)](../sources/1993-03-01-calvo-capital-inflows.md).
