# Carmen M. Reinhart

## Overview

Carmen M. Reinhart was an Economist in the Research Department of the [International Monetary Fund](international-monetary-fund.md) when this paper was written.

## Positions and statements

- 1993-03: With [Guillermo A. Calvo](guillermo-calvo.md) and [Leonardo Leiderman](leonardo-leiderman.md), argued that foreign factors explain about half of monthly real-exchange-rate forecast-error variance for most of the ten Latin American countries in the 1988–91 sample. Source: [Calvo, Leiderman, and Reinhart, capital inflows (1993)](../sources/1993-03-01-calvo-capital-inflows.md).
