# International Monetary Fund

## Overview

The International Monetary Fund published *IMF Staff Papers*. [Guillermo A. Calvo](guillermo-calvo.md) and [Carmen M. Reinhart](carmen-reinhart.md) were in its Research Department when they wrote, with [Leonardo Leiderman](leonardo-leiderman.md), on the 1990–91 capital-inflow episode.

## Positions and statements

- 1993-03: *IMF Staff Papers* carried the argument that Latin American capital inflows and real appreciation in 1990–91 were largely an external shock, and that the available policy tools could soften a reversal rather than undo the appreciation. Source: [Calvo, Leiderman, and Reinhart, capital inflows (1993)](../sources/1993-03-01-calvo-capital-inflows.md).
