# Leonardo Leiderman

## Overview

Leonardo Leiderman was a Professor in the Department of Economics at Tel Aviv University, and a Visiting Scholar in the Research Department of the [International Monetary Fund](international-monetary-fund.md), when this paper was written.

## Positions and statements

- 1993-03: With [Guillermo A. Calvo](guillermo-calvo.md) and [Carmen M. Reinhart](carmen-reinhart.md), argued that external factors, especially US conditions, account for a large part of the 1990–91 capital inflows and real appreciation in Latin America. Source: [Calvo, Leiderman, and Reinhart, capital inflows (1993)](../sources/1993-03-01-calvo-capital-inflows.md).
