# Real exchange rate

## Definition

In this paper, the real exchange rate appreciates when a larger transfer from abroad raises spending on nontraded goods and their relative price rises.

## Evidence

- The 1990–91 capital inflows to Latin America were accompanied by real appreciation in most of the countries studied. Source: [Calvo, Leiderman, and Reinhart, capital inflows (1993)](../sources/1993-03-01-calvo-capital-inflows.md).
- For most of ten countries, foreign factors account for about half of the monthly forecast-error variance of the real exchange rate over January 1988–November 1991. The share is larger where domestic policy was stable. Source: [Calvo, Leiderman, and Reinhart, capital inflows (1993)](../sources/1993-03-01-calvo-capital-inflows.md).
